## Overview

**Shanya Academy** (山崖书院) is a prominent Confucian academy in the Haoran Heaven, deeply intertwined with the fate of the Wen Sheng lineage (文圣一脉). Originally situated in the Great Li Dynasty (大骊王朝) and recognized as one of the orthodox Seventy-Two Academies, it underwent a period of severe political and academic suppression. This culminated in its physical relocation to the Great Sui Dynasty (大隋王朝) and the temporary loss of its prestigious Seventy-Two Academies title. 

Despite these setbacks, the academy remained a vital stronghold for the teachings of the Wen Sheng lineage. Guided by figures like Qi Jingchun (齐静春), Mao Xiaodong (茅小冬), and eventually the Great Li National Teacher Cui Chan (崔瀺), Shanya Academy weathered profound existential crises to maintain its academic heritage and influence across the northern continents.

## History & Relocation

### The Great Li Era & Suppression
In its early days, Shanya Academy was established in the Great Li Dynasty. Its inaugural Mountain Chief set a defining academic preamble, advocating that the traditional Confucian pillars of "learning, questioning, thinking, and discerning" (学问思辨) must ultimately be grounded in the concept of "action" (行). This closely mirrored the practical "merit and action" (事功) philosophy of the Wen Sheng lineage. 

However, under the leadership of the sage Qi Jingchun, the academy faced intense pressure. Because the Wen Sheng lineage had fallen out of favor in the central Confucian Temple, the scholarly atmosphere in Great Li heavily favored the rival Guanhu Academy (观湖书院). In a remarkable display of selflessness and pragmatism, Qi Jingchun did not force his students to stay; instead, he personally wrote recommendation letters for local scholars, facilitating their smooth transfer to Guanhu Academy.

Simultaneously, Cui Chan, who had ostensibly betrayed the Wen Sheng lineage to become the Great Li National Teacher, orchestrated a complex plot against Qi Jingchun. Cui Chan viewed Shanya Academy as a "chicken rib" (鸡肋) and proposed building a new, more compliant academy at Piyun Mountain (披云山) to replace it, intending to completely sever Qi Jingchun's academic incense.

### Relocation to Great Sui
Following Qi Jingchun's sacrificial death in the Lizhu Grotto-Heaven, Shanya Academy was physically and institutionally relocated to the capital of the Great Sui Dynasty, specifically onto **Donghua Mountain** (东华山). The Great Sui, known for its flourishing literary tradition, provided a temporary sanctuary. During this perilous transition, Qi Jingchun's junior martial brother, Mao Xiaodong, assumed the role of Vice Mountain Chief, single-handedly sheltering the academy's remaining scholars and maintaining its operational integrity against local hostilities. The academy's original site in the Great Li Dynasty was subsequently repurposed to build the new capital's Confucian Temple.

### Resurgence under Cui Chan
Years later, the political landscape shifted dramatically. Cui Chan formally assumed the title of Mountain Chief of Shanya Academy. Under his overarching strategic vision and the rising hegemony of the Great Li Dynasty, the academy's fame skyrocketed. Cui Chan explicitly demanded that Shanya Academy be reinstated into the ranks of the Seventy-Two Academies. While orthodox Confucian factions initially resisted, arguing that the academy's newfound prestige relied entirely on Cui Chan's political power rather than genuine scholarly output, the undeniable effectiveness of the Wen Sheng lineage's philosophy during the Haoran-Manhuang War ultimately forced the world to reevaluate the academy's status.

## Hierarchy & Notable Figures

*   **First Mountain Chief:** Established the academy's core philosophical tenet of grounding scholarship in practical action.
*   **Qi Jingchun (齐静春):** The former Mountain Chief who oversaw the academy during its most suppressed era in Great Li. He was beloved by his students but faced insurmountable political opposition, eventually sacrificing himself to save the Lizhu Grotto-Heaven.
*   **Mao Xiaodong (茅小冬):** The Vice Mountain Chief (副山长) who acted as the de facto leader during the academy's relocation to Great Sui. A towering, uncompromising scholar, he successfully defended the academy's lineage (文脉) against Great Sui's covert attacks.
*   **Cui Chan (崔瀺):** The Great Li National Teacher who later claimed the title of Mountain Chief. He utilized the academy as a crucial ideological base to propagate his "merit and action" philosophy across the northern continents.
*   **Li Baoping (李宝瓶):** A standout student of the academy. Originally a young girl from the Lizhu Grotto-Heaven chosen by Qi Jingchun as his academic successor, she traveled to the relocated Shanya Academy and grew into a formidable Confucian scholar renowned for her relentless, logic-driven debate style.

## Teachings / Doctrine

Shanya Academy's core doctrine deviates from the purely theoretical discourses of some orthodox academies. It strictly adheres to the principle that all scholarship must be tested and realized through **"action" (行)** and practical governance. This ideological stance made the academy a controversial but ultimately indispensable institution, particularly when the Haoran Heaven required pragmatic leaders and military strategists to survive the apocalyptic invasion from the Manhuang Heaven.
