## Overview

The **Sub-Sage Lineage** (亚圣一脉) is a massive, highly orthodox academic and political super-faction within the Confucian Temple (文庙) of the Haoran Heaven. Founded by the Sub-Sage (亚圣), this lineage serves as one of the paramount pillars of Confucian doctrine, exerting staggering influence over both secular dynasties and mountain cultivation sects across multiple continents.

Historically, the Sub-Sage Lineage engaged in a fierce ideological and academic conflict with the Wen Sheng Lineage (文圣一脉) known as the "Three-Four Dispute" (三四之争). The Sub-Sage emerged victorious in this debate, cementing his lineage's status as the supreme orthodox teaching of the era and resulting in the temporary removal of the Wen Sheng's statues from Confucian temples. The lineage is renowned for producing an overwhelming number of elite scholars, commanding immense respect and authority in the central temple assemblies.

## Core Philosophy

The doctrines of the Sub-Sage Lineage stand in stark contrast to the pragmatic "Merit and Action" and "Human Nature is Evil" philosophies of the Wen Sheng Lineage. Its foundational teachings are centered around moral purity and innate virtue:

*   **Human Nature is Good (性本善):** The lineage fundamentally believes that humanity is born with an inherent, unblemished goodness. 
*   **The Four Hearts (四心学说):** To maintain and cultivate this innate goodness, the lineage emphasizes the "Four Hearts"—compassion (恻隐), shame and dislike of evil (羞恶), respect and reverence (恭敬), and the ability to distinguish right from wrong (是非).
*   **Righteousness (义):** While the Most Holy Teacher focuses on Benevolence (仁) and the Ritual Sage focuses on Propriety/Rules (礼), the Sub-Sage's core academic foundation is built upon Righteousness (义), advocating for moral absolute correctness and justice in the governance of the world.

## Hierarchy & Notable Figures

The Sub-Sage Lineage boasts a vast and robust hierarchy, dominating many of the Haoran Heaven's highest academic positions.

*   **The Sub-Sage (亚圣):** The founding patriarch. A supreme 14th-Realm entity who merged his Dao with the Middle Earth Divine Continent, anchoring the center of the human world.
*   **Yuan Pang (元雱):** A direct disciple (嫡传弟子) of the Sub-Sage. Originally hailing from the Qingming Heaven, Yuan Pang possesses extraordinary intellect and became the youngest academy head in the history of the Confucian Temple.
*   **Chen Chun'an (陈淳安):** A towering figure in the South Posuo Continent known as the "Pure Confucian" (醇儒). He represents the absolute moral pinnacle of the lineage's teachings, ultimately sacrificing his life to hold the line against the Manhuang Heaven invasion.
*   **A Liang (阿良):** An extreme outlier within the lineage. A legendary wandering sword cultivator who is universally recognized as the most unorthodox and "un-scholarly" student of the Sub-Sage lineage, though his combat prowess is virtually unmatched.

## Associated Academies

The Sub-Sage Lineage controls or heavily influences numerous orthodox academies among the Confucian Seventy-Two Academies. Notable institutions include:

*   **Shanlu Academy (山麓书院):** Located in the South Posuo Continent, this academy acts as a major stronghold for the Sub-Sage Lineage's teachings in the southern hemisphere, deeply associated with Chen Chun'an.
*   **Hengqu Academy (横渠书院):** The academy where the young prodigy Yuan Pang was appointed as the mountain chief (山长), serving as a central hub for the lineage's modern academic propagation.
