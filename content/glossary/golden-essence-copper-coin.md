## Definition

**Golden Essence Copper Coin** (金精铜钱) is a high-value denomination of [Immortal Money](/glossary/immortal-money), the spiritual currency of the cultivation world of Jian Lai.

## Context / Usage

Golden Essence Copper Coins are associated with significant transactions and the strengthening of treasures — for example, they are described as being consumed when upgrading [Moon in the Well](/artifacts/moon-in-the-well). They belong to the same currency system as the [Snowflake Coin](/glossary/snowflake-coin) and the [Grain Rain Coin](/glossary/grain-rain-coin). Exact conversion ratios are not recorded here, pending source confirmation.
