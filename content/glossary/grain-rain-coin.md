## Definition

**Grain Rain Coin** (谷雨钱) is one of the denominations of [Immortal Money](/glossary/immortal-money), the spiritual currency circulated among cultivators in the world of Jian Lai.

## Context / Usage

The Grain Rain Coin belongs to the same system of cultivator denominations as the [Snowflake Coin](/glossary/snowflake-coin) and the [Golden Essence Copper Coin](/glossary/golden-essence-copper-coin). The precise value and conversion ratios between these denominations are not recorded here, pending source confirmation.
