## Definition

**Snowflake Coin** (雪花钱) is one of the denominations of [Immortal Money](/glossary/immortal-money), the spiritual currency circulated among cultivators in the world of Jian Lai. It is used in everyday transactions within the cultivation economy.

## Context / Usage

As a unit of Immortal Money, the Snowflake Coin sits within a broader system of cultivator denominations alongside the [Grain Rain Coin](/glossary/grain-rain-coin) and the [Golden Essence Copper Coin](/glossary/golden-essence-copper-coin). The exact conversion rates between these denominations are not recorded here, pending source confirmation.
