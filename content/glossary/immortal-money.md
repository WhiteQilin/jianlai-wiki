## Definition

**Immortal Money** (神仙钱) is the umbrella term for the spiritual currencies circulated among cultivators in the world of Jian Lai. Unlike ordinary mortal coinage, immortal money holds value within the cultivation economy and is used for high-stakes transactions, the purchase of rare materials, and the upgrading of treasures.

## Context / Usage

Immortal money underpins the cultivation economy. It is the medium through which sects and wealthy factions conduct major dealings — for example, the exorbitant sums exchanged in the practice of "betting on porcelain" to acquire [Natal Porcelain](/glossary/natal-porcelain). Resources of comparable value, such as the Golden Essence Copper Coins consumed when upgrading [Moon in the Well](/artifacts/moon-in-the-well), illustrate how the immortal economy ties wealth directly to cultivation power.
