## Overview

*Caged Sparrow* (笼中雀) is the first natal flying sword awakened by Chen Ping'an during his time at the Sword Qi Great Wall. Unconventional for a sword cultivator, its innate divine ability (本命神通) does not focus on direct piercing lethality or extreme speed. Instead, it specializes in absolute spatial isolation and territorial control. When manifested, the flying sword creates an independent Small Heaven and Earth (小天地) that traps enemies within its borders, subjecting them to localized rules and cutting them off from the outside world.

## Origin and Naming

The flying sword naturally awakened within Chen Ping'an's acupoints at the Sword Qi Great Wall. It received its name from the Eldest Sword Immortal, Chen Qingdu. During an impromptu test where Chen Qingdu brought Chen Ping'an into a martial arts manifestation, Chen Ping'an instinctively deployed his newly awakened flying sword, trapping Chen Qingdu's avatar within the nascent inner world. Amused by the young man's method of isolating him, Chen Qingdu looked around the constructed cage and praised it, remarking, "Trapped within, what a caged sparrow" (置身其中，好一个笼中雀). Chen Ping'an subsequently adopted this phrase as the flying sword's true name.

## Mechanisms and Progression

The core mechanism of *Caged Sparrow* is the manifestation of a sealed, self-contained spatial domain that temporarily overrides the natural laws of the outside world.

*   **Spatial Manipulation:** The scale of the domain can be dynamically adjusted according to Chen Ping'an's needs and cultivation realm. By deliberately shrinking the domain's perimeter into a claustrophobic space, the localized spatial rules and restrictions become incredibly dense. This active compression heavily suppresses the trapped enemy's spiritual energy and physical movements.
*   **Cosmological Synergy:** *Caged Sparrow* corresponds to the cosmological concept of "Yu" (宇)—the spatial directions of heaven, earth, and the four cardinal points. It perfectly synergizes with Chen Ping'an's second natal flying sword, *Moon in the Well* (井中月), which represents "Zhou" (宙)—the flow of time. In this pairing, *Caged Sparrow* acts as the spatial "shell" or framework, while *Moon in the Well* acts as the "flesh and blood," filling the isolated space with countless microscopic sword arrays and manipulating the internal flow of time.
*   **Refinement and Upgrading:** To elevate the grade of *Caged Sparrow* and perfect its internal laws—such as increasing the maximum size of the domain—the flying sword must actively consume and refine Dragon Slaying Stone (斩龙石), an exceedingly rare and priceless material.

## Combat Applications and Weaknesses

The spatial cage provides a massive tactical advantage for Chen Ping'an's pure martial arts foundation. By trapping enemies within a defined perimeter, it prevents them from using fleeing techniques or keeping their distance, forcing them into devastating close-quarters fist combat. 

However, the domain has distinct vulnerabilities:
1.  **High Consumption:** Maintaining and manipulating the Small Heaven and Earth exerts a massive, continuous toll on the user's mental energy and spiritual Qi.
2.  **Realm Suppression:** The domain can be forcibly shattered by cultivators wielding overwhelming realm superiority or devastating martial force, as demonstrated when the legendary sword cultivator Pei Min successfully broke through the spatial cage with his umbrella sword.
3.  **Natural Counters:** Certain specialized flying swords possess innate abilities specifically designed to ignore or destroy spatial boundaries. For example, Liu Cai's flying sword *Bi Luo* (碧落) acts as a perfect cosmological counter, capable of effortlessly piercing and shattering the spatial cage of *Caged Sparrow*.
