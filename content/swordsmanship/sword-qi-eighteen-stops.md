## Overview

The Sword Qi Eighteen Stops (剑气十八停) is a foundational qi-circulation method (运气法门) designed specifically for sword cultivators to manage and refine sword qi within their bodies. Known for its rigorous demands and its profound benefits to a sword cultivator's foundation, the technique stands as one of the most highly regarded physiological and energetic circulation methods in the universe. 

## History and Transmission

The method was originally passed down by the legendary sword cultivator A'Liang. During its early years in the Sword Qi Great Wall, the technique was highly restricted and exclusive. Only a handful of exceptionally talented young sword cultivators were permitted and capable of practicing this method, most notably Ning Yao, Chen Sanqiu, and Die Zhang. 

## Improvement and Publication

Following the apocalyptic war with the Wild World, the establishment of the Five-Colored Heaven, and the relocation of Feisheng City, the status of the technique underwent a monumental shift. The Hidden Official lineage, responsible for overseeing the future of the surviving sword cultivators, introduced a refined and improved version of the method known as the "New Eighteen Stops" (新十八停). 

In a departure from traditional cultivation world practices—where high-tier methods are jealously guarded as sect secrets—the Hidden Official lineage made the New Eighteen Stops entirely public. The method was openly disseminated to all sword cultivators residing in Feisheng City, allowing any practitioner within the city's jurisdiction to cultivate the technique and strengthen their foundational sword qi.
