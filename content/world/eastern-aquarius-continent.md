## Overview

The **Eastern Aquarius Continent** (宝瓶洲) is one of the nine major continents of the Haoran Heaven (浩然天下). Despite being the smallest of the nine continents in terms of territorial size and historically derided by outsiders as a cultural and martial backwater, it achieved unprecedented prominence by becoming the only continent in the world to be entirely unified under a single secular empire: the Great Li Dynasty (大骊王朝). 

During the apocalyptic Haoran-Manhuang War, when neighboring southern continents quickly fell to the invading Monster Race, the Eastern Aquarius Continent stood firm. Under the strategic mastery of National Teacher Cui Chan (崔瀺), it served as the ultimate defensive bulwark that successfully shattered the monster army's momentum, saving the Haoran Heaven from total subjugation.

## Geography

Located in the northern hemisphere of the Haoran Heaven, the Eastern Aquarius Continent lies south of Beiju Luzhou (北俱芦洲) and north of the Tongye Continent (桐叶洲). Historically, the continent was roughly divided into distinct northern, central, and southern regions, comprising dozens of independent mortal dynasties and vassal states.

Following the Haoran-Manhuang War, the continent's geography and destiny were drastically reshaped. The most significant geographical feature is the **Qi River** (齐渎), a massive, artificially excavated continental river (大渎) that spans across the landmass. The creation of the Qi River fundamentally altered the continent's water transport, dragon veins, and overall destiny, anchoring its newfound status as a major power in the world.

## Ruling Factions & Sects

The continent's power structure underwent a radical transformation from a fractured landscape of numerous sects and countries into a highly centralized, militarized machine.

*   **The Great Li Dynasty (大骊王朝):** The undisputed hegemon of the continent. Through a mix of overwhelming military force via its Iron Cavalry and ruthless political maneuvering, Great Li absorbed every other nation, achieving the historical feat of "one continent, one nation" (一洲即一国).
*   **Confucian Academies:** The orthodox moral and cultural governance of the continent is overseen by three major Confucian academies: Guanhu Academy (观湖书院), Shanya Academy (山崖书院), and Linlu Academy (林鹿书院).
*   **Major Sects and Factions:** Despite the Great Li Dynasty's absolute secular control, the continent hosts several immensely powerful cultivation factions that serve as its defensive pillars. These include:
    *   The Daoist **Divine Hao Sect** (神诰宗)
    *   The Military School ancestral mountains **True Wu Mountain** (真武山) and **Wind Snow Temple** (风雪庙)
    *   The Sword Cultivator sects **Zheng Yang Mountain** (正阳山) and **Wind Thunder Garden** (风雷园)
    *   The **Longquan Sword Sect** (龙泉剑宗), acting as the chief enshrine faction for Great Li.
    *   **Old Dragon City** (老龙城), the wealthiest merchant hub and crucial southern port.
    *   **Zhenjing Sect** (真境宗) at Shujian Lake (书简湖).

## History & Related Events

### The Unification of the Continent
For most of its history, the Eastern Aquarius Continent was fragmented. The turning point came when the Great Li Dynasty, guided by the "Practicality" (事功) ideology of its National Teacher Cui Chan, began a systematic southward conquest. Utilizing military supremacy, espionage, and the monopolized talents from the Lizhu Grotto-Heaven, Great Li steadily conquered peaceful nations like Huangting Country and powerful empires like the Zhuying Dynasty, ultimately unifying the entire continent under the Song surname.

### The Haoran-Manhuang War
When the Sword Qi Great Wall fell and the Manhuang Heaven invaded, the Monster Race swiftly overran Fuyao and Tongye continents before launching a massive assault on the Eastern Aquarius Continent, beginning at Old Dragon City in the south. However, Cui Chan had spent centuries turning the entire continent into an interlocking defensive fortress. By mobilizing every mortal soldier and mountain cultivator, and utilizing墨家 (Mohist) Sword Boats and Mountain-Moving Ferries, the Eastern Aquarius Continent successfully halted the apocalypse. The continent suffered heavy casualties but emerged as the savior of the Haoran Heaven.

## Notable Figures

*   **Cui Chan (崔瀺):** The mastermind behind the continent's unification and its successful defense against the Manhuang Heaven.
*   **Song Changjing (宋长镜):** The supreme military commander of the Great Li Dynasty and an unparalleled 11th-realm pure martial artist.
*   **Chen Ping'an (陈平安):** A native of the continent (from the Lizhu Grotto-Heaven) who eventually succeeded his senior brother Cui Chan as the National Teacher of the Great Li Dynasty.
*   **Qi Jingchun (齐静春):** The former sitting Sage of the Lizhu Grotto-Heaven and the founder of Shanya Academy, whose profound influence shaped the continent's destiny.
