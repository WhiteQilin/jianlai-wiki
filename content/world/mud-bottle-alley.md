## Overview

Mud Bottle Alley (泥瓶巷) is a poor residential alley within the [Lizhu Grotto-Heaven](/world/lizhu-grotto-heaven), best known as the childhood home of the protagonist [Chen Ping'an](/characters/chen-pingan). The alley sits among the humble quarters of the small town, far removed from the wealth and influence of the visiting cultivation sects.

## Significance

As Chen Ping'an's birthplace and early home, Mud Bottle Alley is the starting point of his journey. The hardship he endured here — surviving as an orphan after his parents' deaths and the shattering of his [Natal Porcelain](/glossary/natal-porcelain) — shaped much of his cautious, frugal, and resilient character.

## Governing Power

The alley lies within the Lizhu Grotto-Heaven, which falls under the administration of the [Great Li Dynasty](/factions/great-li-dynasty).
