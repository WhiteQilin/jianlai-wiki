## Overview

The Qingming Top Ten (青冥天下十人) is the preeminent ranking of the ten most powerful and influential cultivators within Qingming Heaven. Historically, this list has been evaluated and published once every century by the Xianzhang Sect (仙杖派) in Yongzhou. For over three hundred years, the core members of the top ten saw very little variation, dominated consistently by the three headmasters of Bai Yujing and a handful of established Daoist patriarchs. 

However, a recently published iteration of the Top Ten list sent shockwaves across Qingming Heaven. Not only did it feature eleven individuals due to an unprecedented tie for the tenth position, but its publisher remained a complete mystery, bypassing the traditional authority of the Xianzhang Sect.

## Impact and Controversy

The publication of this specific list caused massive unrest across the fourteen states of Qingming Heaven for several key reasons:

*   **The Exclusion of Kou Ming:** For the first time in history, the Great Headmaster of Bai Yujing, Kou Ming (寇名), was completely excluded from the list, seemingly confirming rumors of his disappearance or hinting at a massive shift in Bai Yujing's power structure.
*   **The Rise of Wu Zhou:** The female crown Wu Zhou, who had been in seclusion for so long that she was nearly forgotten by the world, dramatically re-entered the rankings at the fourth position, displacing the old guard like Gao Gu. This sparked immediate speculation that she had ascended to the Fourteenth Realm. 
*   **The Tie at Tenth:** By forcing a tie at the tenth position, the anonymous authors of the list deliberately placed Xuandu Temple's Wang Sun in the spotlight. Placing a second member of Xuandu Temple in the Top Ten—a sect historically at odds with Bai Yujing—was widely perceived as an open provocation meant to stir conflict. 
*   **Martial Artists on the List:** Unusually for a realm dominated by Daoist cultivators, two pure martial artists—Lin Jiangxian and Xin Ku—secured spots on the list, reflecting the formidable, transcendent martial power capable of rivaling the pinnacle of Daoist cultivation.
