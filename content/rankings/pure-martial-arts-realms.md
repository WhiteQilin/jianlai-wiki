## Overview

The Pure Martial Arts Realms (纯粹武夫境界) represent the rigorous, physically demanding cultivation ladder for pure martial artists in the *Jian Lai* universe. Distinct from the ethereal and spell-driven paths of Qi refiners and sword cultivators, pure martial arts emphasizes the extreme tempering of the physical body, the consolidation of pure true qi (纯粹真气), and the forging of an indomitable martial intent (拳意). 

By strictly refining their flesh, bones, and spirit, high-tier martial artists can unleash devastating physical force capable of shattering magical domains, tearing through sword arrays, and rivaling the most powerful immortals.

## Criteria and Scope

The ranking encompasses the sequential progression of martial arts from the foundational stages to the absolute pinnacle of human physical potential. Advancement through these realms is notoriously difficult, requiring immense willpower and physical endurance. While Qi refiners progress through fluid accumulations of spiritual energy, martial artists face hard, physical bottlenecks. Breaking through major thresholds—particularly moving from the 6th to the 7th realm—is known to frequently trigger heaven and earth phenomena (天地异象), allowing the martial artist to borrow external natural forces to further temper their physique and soul.

## Progression and Mechanics

### Foundational Training
The earliest stages of martial arts rely heavily on physical discipline and breath control. The core foundational exercises include the standing stance (站桩), the walking stance (走桩), and the sleeping stance (睡桩). These methods, particularly the sleeping stance, focus heavily on the accumulation of energy within the acupoints and the smooth circulation of breath. 

### The Stopping Realm (10th Realm)
For a long time in history, the 10th Realm was known as the Stopping Realm (止境), signifying the supposed absolute limit of martial arts. However, reaching this realm is not a single step; it is internally subdivided into three distinct, increasingly difficult layers:
1.  **Qi-abundant (气盛):** The initial entry into the Stopping Realm, where the martial artist's pure true qi reaches an overwhelming density.
2.  **Return-to-true (归真):** The intermediate layer, representing a refinement and internalization of martial intent and physical resilience.
3.  **Spirit-arrived (神到):** The pinnacle layer of the Stopping Realm. Achieving even "half a step" into Spirit-arrived grants a martial artist a golden, god-like physical constitution and a fist aura that glows like thick water.

### The Eleventh Realm
The 11th Realm (武道十一境) shatters the ceiling of the Stopping Realm. It is an almost mythical state of existence, achieved only by unparalleled martial geniuses (such as Song Changjing, Pei Bei, Xin Ku, and Chen Ping'an). When martial artists break into this realm, their martial intent can visibly manifest across the world, projecting their towering figures at the highest mountain peaks of the universe.

## Disputed / Unverified Placements

Due to the limited worldview of lower-level practitioners, early historical records and colloquial knowledge sometimes mistakenly referred to the 9th Realm (Mountain Peak Realm) as the "Stopping Realm Grandmaster" (止境宗师), believing that hovering over mountains was the absolute end of the path. This misconception is cleared up as practitioners encounter true 10th and 11th Realm entities.

Additionally, while the exact numbering and mechanical thresholds of realms 3, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11 are strictly documented in the archives, the specific nomenclature for the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th, and 6th realms requires further source verification to complete the comprehensive 1-11 ladder.

## References

* **剑来1：少年起微末，第七章 — 拳谱:** Details the foundational training methods of pure martial arts, including the standing, walking, and sleeping stances.
* **剑来2：忽为远行客，第十一章 — 拜山头:** Provides early colloquial definitions of the 8th Realm (Feathering/Far-traveling) and the 9th Realm (Mountain Peak).
* **剑来3：清梦压星河，第六章 — 陆地剑仙:** Confirms the existence of the 10th Realm (Stopping Realm) and the 11th Realm for top-tier martial artists like Song Changjing.
* **剑来6：剑符在扁舟，第八章 — 传道人传道:** Explains the mechanics of breaking through martial arts bottlenecks, noting that breaking from the 6th to the 7th realm frequently triggers heaven and earth phenomena.
* **剑来18：我与我周旋，第八章 — 入海处遇故人:** Explicitly names the 3rd Realm as the Willow-muscle Realm (柳筋境) and describes it as a notorious "retaining realm" bottleneck.
* **剑来38：请君入梦来，第一章 — 行刑:** Details the internal subdivisions of the 10th Stopping Realm into Qi-abundant (气盛), Return-to-true (归真), and Spirit-arrived (神到).
* **剑来46：人间半部书，第六章 — 早知会被仙字误:** Confirms the 7th Realm as the Golden Body Realm (金身境), the 8th as Far-traveling, and the 9th as Mountain Peak. 
* **剑来52：吾有辞乡剑，第一章 — 于混沌一片中:** Depicts the awe-inspiring phenomenon of multiple martial artists simultaneously breaking into the legendary 11th Realm (武道十一境) and manifesting their forms at the mountain peak.
