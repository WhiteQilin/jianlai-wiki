## Overview

The Several Worlds Young Ten Persons (数座天下年轻十人) is a prestigious, cosmological ranking that identifies the ten most exceptionally talented young cultivators across all known heavens (including Haoran Heaven, Qingming Heaven, and the Wild World). The list serves as a definitive benchmark for the highest potential of the younger generation, ignoring traditional boundaries of geography, sect affiliation, and even the distinction between Qi refiners, sword cultivators, and pure martial artists. 

To be included on this list is a mark of supreme Dao destiny and unparalleled talent. The individuals on this list are considered the future pinnacle combatants and leaders of their respective worlds. 

## The List

The exact internal ordering of the Young Ten is rarely publicized, treating the members as a collective pinnacle tier. The known members include:

## Candidate Ten (候补十人)

Directly beneath the primary list is the "Candidate Ten" (候补十人), a secondary roster tracking the next tier of exceptional prodigies who have the potential to break into the main ten. 

One confirmed member of the Candidate Ten is **Shu Zhongshu** (蜀中暑), a young cultivator from the Tianyu Grotto-Heaven in the Liuxia Continent, renowned for having already constructed a Chaoran Pavilion (超然台).

## Notable Placements and Impact

The publication and recognition of this list caused significant ripples across the cultivation worlds due to several shocking inclusions:
*   **Liu Cai's Golden Core Anomaly:** While prodigies like Ning Yao, Cao Ci, and Shan Qing were widely recognized and possessed sufficiently high realms, Liu Cai's inclusion shocked Haoran Heaven. He was merely a Golden Core (Jindan) sword cultivator at the time. His placement was entirely justified by the overwhelming lethality of his two natal flying swords, *Bi Luo* and *Bai Ju*.
*   **Cross-World Mobility:** The list highlights the fluidity of supreme talents across cosmological boundaries, such as Yuan Pang originating from Qingming Heaven but becoming the Asian Sage's disciple in Haoran Heaven.

## References

* **剑来21：皆是笼中雀，第四章 — 炼剑:** Confirms She Yue (赊月) as one of the young ten persons representing the Wild World.
* **剑来25：天地皆同力，第二章 — 又一年五月初五:** Details the shock of Haoran Heaven regarding Liu Cai's inclusion on the list despite only being a Golden Core sword cultivator, comparing his lethal potential to established high-realm prodigies like Ning Yao, Cao Ci, and Shan Qing.
* **剑来25：天地皆同力，第四章 — 十四境修士:** Confirms Bai Yujing's Shan Qing (the Dao Ancestor's closed-door disciple) and the young monk with the twelve-ring tin staff as members of the young ten. Also introduces the existence of the Candidate Ten (候补十人), explicitly naming Shu Zhongshu of Tianyu Grotto-Heaven.
* **剑来28：清都山水郎，第十章 — 压压惊:** Mentions Yuan Pang as one of the young ten across several worlds, noting his Qingming Heaven origins and his status as the Asian Sage's direct disciple.
* **剑来29：座中皆豪杰，第八章 — 那就打:** Confirms Feiran, the head of the Tuoyue Mountain Hundred Sword Immortals, as a member of the young ten.
* **剑来50：蜉蝣见青天，第九章 — 不知天高地厚:** Notes that Liu Cai's first major recognition in the world was his inclusion in the ranking of the Several Worlds Young Ten Persons.
