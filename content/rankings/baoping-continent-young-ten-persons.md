## Overview

The Baoping Continent Young Ten Persons (宝瓶洲年轻十人) is a regional ranking formally proposed by the influential mountain publications (邸报) of the Northern Julu Continent. It aims to evaluate and rank the ten most talented and promising young cultivators native to the Baoping Continent. 

Historically, the Northern Julu Continent's cultivation world looked down upon the Baoping Continent, often dismissing it as a shallow pond incapable of producing true "dragons." However, following a series of shocking battlefield achievements by Baoping Continent youths, the chief writers of the Northern Julu publications changed their stance. They announced the compilation of this specific top-ten list to directly compare the Baoping Continent's emerging prodigies against the Northern Julu Continent's own new generation of top ten youths.

## Criteria and Scope

The list strictly evaluates the younger generation of cultivators based on their raw combat prowess, cultivation speed, and lethal achievements on the battlefield. It explicitly excludes older, established prodigies (such as Song Changjing or Wei Jin) due to age and realm criteria, focusing purely on the emerging youth. 

To be considered for the top spot, a young cultivator must demonstrate the ability to effortlessly leapfrog realms in combat and crush established powerhouses, proving they possess the supreme Dao destiny to eventually open their own sects or influence the cosmological landscape.

## The List

Because the list was heavily teased and iteratively compiled by foreign publications, the exact and complete enumeration of all ten members fluctuates in public discourse. The currently known prominent members include:

## Disputed / Unverified Placements

While Ma Kuxuan's position at the absolute pinnacle of this specific regional list is undisputed—cemented by his effortless slaying of powerful Golden Core sword cultivators in the Zhu Ying Dynasty—the exact ranking numbers for the remaining nine entries are fiercely debated. 

Names such as the Dragon Spring Sword Sect's Xie Ling and the True Realm Sect's Sui Youbian are frequently grouped into the "Listed" or "Candidate" tiers by the reading public. A complete, universally accepted publication of ranks 2 through 10 requires further verification from the Northern Julu Continent's historical mountain archives.
