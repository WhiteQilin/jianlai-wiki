## Overview

A **Sword-Nurturing Gourd** (养剑葫) is a treasure used by sword cultivators to store and slowly nourish flying swords. Rather than serving as a weapon in itself, it acts as a portable vessel in which a flying sword can be sheltered and gradually strengthened. The most prominent example in the series is the vermilion gourd carried at the waist of [Chen Ping'an](/characters/chen-pingan).

## Function

The gourd provides an internal environment suited to housing a flying sword, allowing the sword to be stored compactly and drawn at need. For a sword cultivator, such a vessel is both a practical storage tool and a means of fostering the long-term growth of a treasured blade.

## Known Owners

- **[Chen Ping'an](/characters/chen-pingan):** Carries a vermilion sword-nurturing gourd hung from his waist, a recurring detail of his appearance.
