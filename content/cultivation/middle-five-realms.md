## Overview

The **Middle Five Realms** (中五境) constitute the second major tier of cultivation for Qi Refiners (练气士). Encompassing the 6th through 10th realms, this stage marks a practitioner's true departure from the foundational [Lower Five Realms](/cultivation/lower-five-realms) and their ascent into the ranks of high-level mountain immortals. 

For the vast majority of ordinary cultivators, reaching the Middle Five Realms is an incredibly difficult threshold. Those who succeed command immense respect and authority within secular dynasties and smaller cultivation sects. When a Middle Five Realms cultivator travels through mortal kingdoms and worldly landscapes, the spiritual disturbance they cause is likened to a flood dragon walking a river (蛟龙走江)—a phenomenon that local mountain and water deities must carefully monitor.

## The Realms

The Middle Five Realms consist of five distinct stages of progression, culminating in the esteemed status of an Earth Immortal.

*   **6th Realm: Cave Dwelling / Dongfu Realm (洞府境):** The entry point into the Middle Five Realms. 
*   **7th Realm: Sea Viewing / Guanhai Realm (观海境):** This realm's name is derived from the poetic sentiment, "I ascend the tower to view the hundred rivers; as they enter the sea, they enter my embrace" (我登楼观百川，入海即入我怀). At this stage, the heaven and earth spiritual energy begins to drastically expand the practitioner's meridians, flowing like rivers into the sea, allowing the energy to condense, elevate, and nourish the physical body, significantly extending the cultivator's lifespan.
*   **8th Realm: Dragon Gate / Longmen Realm (龙门境):** The final transitional stage before achieving Earth Immortal status.
*   **9th Realm: Golden Core / Jindan Realm (金丹境):** The breakthrough into this realm is a monumental achievement, often triggering heaven and earth phenomena (天地异象). Upon successfully forming a Golden Core, the cultivator officially becomes an **Earth Immortal** (地仙). In ordinary nations across continents like the Eastern Aquarius Continent, a Golden Core Earth Immortal is considered a virtually unreachable, transcendent existence.
*   **10th Realm: Nascent Soul / Yuanying Realm (元婴境):** The pinnacle of the Middle Five Realms. A Nascent Soul cultivator possesses a profound foundational stability characterized as "immovable as a mountain" (不动如山). 

### Earth Immortals (地仙)
The final two realms of the Middle Five Realms—Jindan (9th) and Yuanying (10th)—are collectively referred to as the Earth Immortal realms (地仙两境). Reaching this level grants extraordinary combat prowess, access to profound divine abilities (such as Yin Spirit excursions / 阴神出窍), and centuries of lifespan. However, they are still bound by certain fundamental laws of heaven and earth.

## Limitations

Despite their immense power, Middle Five Realms cultivators (even 10th Realm Yuanying cultivators) share a critical limitation that separates them from the Upper Five Realms. 

When traveling outside of spiritually dense environments like Grotto-Heavens and Blessed Lands (洞天福地), they must constantly ward off the impure, murky qi of the mortal world. If they are not careful, this worldly impurity can pour into their bodies like a river flowing backward (江河倒灌), eroding their physique and damaging their Dao foundation. Only upon breaking through the Yuanying bottleneck and entering the 11th Realm (Upper Five Realms) do cultivators become truly impervious to this worldly corruption, gaining total freedom of movement across the world.
