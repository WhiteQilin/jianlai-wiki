## Overview

As the Wild World invasion decimated Tongye, Fuyao, and Jinjia Continents, a massive demonic army pivoted to threaten the South Posuo Continent. Rather than immediately launching a full-scale amphibious assault, the demon forces began a terrifying display of geographical manipulation: they continuously hurled countless Wild World mountains into the ocean to pave artificial land bridges, staging their troops a thousand miles off the coast of South Posuo Continent for a crushing, continent-wide siege. The defense of this vital southern flank was orchestrated by the continent's most revered figure, the pure Confucian scholar Chen Chun'an, famously known as the scholar who "shoulders the sun and moon."

Bolstered by reinforcements from other continents—including the Sword Qi Great Wall's peak sword immortal Lu Zhi and the Huai family patriarch from Middle Earth—Chen Chun'an established an impregnable defensive line. The Throne Great Demon Liu Cha, acting as a vanguard for the invasion, directly challenged Chen Chun'an in a targeted clash designed to break the continent's spirit. However, despite the apocalyptic pressure and the calculated machinations of the Wild World's mastermind Zhou Mi, Chen Chun'an and his forces held the line, turning the coastal waters into a tense, deadlocked battleground.

To maintain this vital stalemate, Chen Chun'an made a grueling personal choice. While scholars safely ensconced in Middle Earth harshly criticized him for adopting a seemingly passive, heavily entrenched defensive posture—expecting him to be the first Ascended-tier martyr to die a glorious, world-shaking death in battle—Chen Chun'an chose the heavier burden of surviving to ensure the continent did not fall. He willingly bore the infamy and relentless criticism of the world, prioritizing the physical preservation of South Posuo's land and people over his own legendary reputation.

## Outcome / Consequences

Chen Chun'an's steadfast defense successfully blocked the Wild World from annexing South Posuo Continent, securing one of the few surviving southern footholds for the Haoran World. By absorbing immense military pressure and frustrating Zhou Mi's optimal invasion strategy, this defensive stand prevented a total regional collapse, preserved the lives of millions, and bought the Haoran World the critical time needed to eventually launch a counter-offensive.
