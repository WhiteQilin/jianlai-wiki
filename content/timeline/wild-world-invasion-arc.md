## Overview

Following the sundering of the Sword Qi Great Wall, the primary barrier between the two worlds was removed, allowing the Wild World's demon armies to surge into the Haoran World. Masterminded by the "Sea of Literature" Wenhai Zhou Mi and the Tuoyue Mountain Grand Ancestor, the invasion was an unprecedented cosmological event aimed at subjugating the human domain. The vanguard forces immediately targeted the continents closest to the southern breach, leveraging sheer numbers and the overwhelming power of the Throne Great Demons to establish geographic footholds. 

Due to a lack of unified preparation and the sudden severity of the demonic onslaught, the southern continents of Tongye, Fuyao, and Jinjia faced catastrophic losses. Lacking a centralized defensive strategy, large swaths of these territories were rapidly overrun, transforming into slaughter grounds and ghost cities as the Wild World sought to systematically devour their mountain and water fortunes. However, as the demon armies pushed further north, they encountered fierce, coordinated resistance from Haoran's remaining strongholds.

At the southern tip of the Bao Ping Continent, Great Li National Master Cui Chan orchestrated a massive, continent-wide defensive line, turning Old Dragon City into a brutal meat grinder to halt the invasion's momentum. Simultaneously, in the South Posuo Continent, the Confucian pure scholar Chen Chun'an physically held the line against the demonic advance. The invasion ultimately devolved into a bloody, multi-continent war of attrition, forcing hidden plots to the surface and compelling legendary figures to sacrifice everything to stabilize the shattered human world.

## Major Timeline Points

*   **The Fall of the Southern Continents:** The rapid and devastating collapse of Tongye, Fuyao, and Jinjia Continents under the initial surge of the Wild World's armies, resulting in massive casualties.
*   **Bai Ye's Sword Strike at Fuyao Continent:** The world's most proud scholar wields the immortal sword Tai Bai against six Throne Great Demons to stall the invasion.
*   **Wanyan Laojing's Betrayal:** An Ascended-tier grand cultivator secretly defects to Zhou Mi, shattering the defense of the Jinjia Continent and sealing its defeat.
*   **The Battle of Old Dragon City:** Cui Chan and the Great Li Dynasty orchestrate a desperate, continent-wide defense at the southern tip of Bao Ping Continent, successfully halting the demonic advance.
*   **Chen Chun'an Defends South Posuo Continent:** The Confucian pure scholar Chen Chun'an physically blocks the demonic advance, securing the southern flank of the Haoran World.
*   **Cui Chan and Qi Jingchun Intercept Zhou Mi:** A spiritual and intellectual confrontation at the Taoye Ferry in Tongye Continent that shatters Zhou Mi's optimal invasion strategy.

## Outcome / Consequences

The Wild World Invasion Arc fundamentally altered the cosmological map of the Haoran World. The invasion inflicted catastrophic casualties and shattered the landscapes of multiple southern continents, plunging the human domain into an unprecedented crisis. However, the desperate defensive stands at Bao Ping and South Posuo ultimately halted the demonic momentum, solidifying Haoran's defensive lines, disrupting Zhou Mi's optimal grand design, and setting the stage for his forced ascension and the eventual Haoran counter-offensive.
