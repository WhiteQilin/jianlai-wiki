## Overview

Following Wenhai Zhou Mi's forced ascension to the ancient heavenly ruins, the apocalyptic invasion into the Haoran World is finally halted, shifting the cosmological balance. To preserve the purest lineage of sword cultivators escaping from the fallen Sword Qi Great Wall, Confucian sages and peak sword immortals cleave open chaos to establish a sanctuary known as the Five-Colored Heaven. This new world guarantees the continuation of the sword cultivators' legacy and introduces a brand-new cosmological domain to the universe.

With the immediate crisis on the southern continents stabilized, the surviving sages, sect leaders, and military heads gather at the Wen Temple in Middle Earth to orchestrate an unprecedented retaliation. The Haoran World officially transitions from a desperate, fragmented defense to a highly coordinated, all-out counter-offensive. By securing four Guixu gates and constructing massive forward-operating ferry cities, Haoran pours troops, sword boats, and supplies directly into the Wild World, turning the demon homeland into the new primary battlefield.

As the counter-offensive rages, epic cosmological clashes reshape the universe. Key human figures launch targeted strikes against the Wild World's foundational anchors, culminating in the physical cleaving of Tuoyue Mountain. Concurrently, ancient threats resurface to challenge the new order, leading to the joint execution of the revived Military Ancestor Jiang She. The arc climaxes when Chen Ping'an and his allies initiate a heaven-shaking confrontation to prevent Zhou Mi from perfectly consolidating his divine authority in the ancient heavenly ruins.

## Outcome / Consequences

The Counter-Offensive & New Heaven Arc marks the Haoran World's successful recovery of the strategic initiative, fundamentally redefining the cosmological map. The Wild World is subjected to a massive retaliatory invasion, the newly established Five-Colored Heaven flourishes as an independent and powerful domain, and Chen Ping'an's role transforms from a localized defender into a central cosmological agent capable of influencing the fate of multiple worlds.
