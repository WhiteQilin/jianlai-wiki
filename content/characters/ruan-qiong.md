## Overview

**Ruan Qiong** (阮邛) is a renowned sword-forging immortal and master blacksmith who came to [Dragon Spring County](/world/dragon-spring-county) (the former [Lizhu Grotto-Heaven](/world/lizhu-grotto-heaven)) to establish the Dragon Spring Forge. A formidable sword cultivator in his own right, he is also one of the most skilled craftsmen in the forging of flying swords and immortal weapons, combining martial prowess with exceptional technical mastery.

## Background

Ruan Qiong originated from the [Sword Qi Great Wall](/world/sword-qi-great-wall) and was sent to Dragon Spring County by the [Great Li Dynasty](/factions/great-li-dynasty) to establish a forge and oversee the region's blacksmithing operations. His arrival and the founding of Dragon Spring Forge had a lasting impact on the local cultivation landscape and economy.

## Significance

As the master blacksmith of Dragon Spring Forge, Ruan Qiong plays a critical role in the cultivation world's weapon supply, particularly for flying swords. His forge became a cornerstone of the area's identity and a key strategic asset for Great Li. He also interacts with [Chen Ping'an](/characters/chen-pingan) early in the protagonist's journey, offering guidance rooted in his own deep martial and craft experience.
