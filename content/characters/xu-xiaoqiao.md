## Overview

Xu Xiaoqiao (徐小桥) is a Golden Core (金丹境) sword cultivator and the true first founding disciple (开山大弟子) of the Dragon Springs Sword Sect (龙泉剑宗) established by Saint Ruan Qiong. Formerly a disciple of Fengxue Temple (风雪庙), she was expelled after committing a grave mistake. She is best known for voluntarily severing the thumb of her dominant sword-holding hand to prove her resolve, restarting her sword path from scratch as a left-handed cultivator. 

## Appearance and Personality

Physically, Xu Xiaoqiao is notable for missing the thumb on her right hand. 

She is a highly diligent cultivator but struggles with profound insecurity and melancholy regarding her future prospects. Because her innate talent and fortune pale in comparison to her fellow disciples, such as Xie Ling and Dong Gu, she often feels a deep sense of inferiority over her arduous Great Dao. Despite this, she is pragmatic, hardworking, and easily moved by genuine kindness, cherishing her bond with Ruan Xiu.

## History

After being expelled from Fengxue Temple, Xu Xiaoqiao sought refuge with Ruan Qiong. Ruan Qiong initially deemed her heart unstable—always seeking a way out—and only allowed her to perform menial labor in his blacksmith shop without officially taking her as a disciple. To prove her dedication, she chopped off her right thumb and began practicing swordsmanship with her left hand.

Although she was Ruan Qiong's first disciple, her slower progress meant Dong Gu reached the Golden Core realm before her, leading others to call her the "Third Senior Sister" while Dong Gu became the Second Senior Brother. She later accompanied Ruan Xiu and a group of Dali officials on a journey southward, acting as a laborer to mine Furong stone from a mountain.

When Liu Xianyang assumed the position of Sect Master, she attended a modest sect dinner where Liu Xianyang jokingly claimed she looked at him with the tender, loving gaze of a real older sister. Infuriated, she threatened to tear his mouth apart. After the Dragon Springs Sword Sect completely relocated its base out of the Chuzhou region, she began taking her own two newly accepted direct disciples out for experiential travel.

## Cultivation

Xu Xiaoqiao is a Golden Core (金丹境) sword cultivator. Her path forward is fraught with difficulties; Ruan Xiu once bluntly informed her that breaking through to the Nascent Soul realm would be significantly harder for her than for Dong Gu, though Ruan Qiong would still aid her when the time came. 

## Relationships

*   **Ruan Qiong:** Her strict master who took her in after she proved her resolve.
*   **Ruan Xiu:** She affectionately calls Ruan Xiu her "Eldest Senior Sister" (大师姐). Ruan Xiu's simple admission that she liked Xu Xiaoqiao a bit more than the others brought Xu Xiaoqiao to tears of gratitude.
*   **Dong Gu:** The Second Senior Brother. Despite feeling slight sorrow over losing her seniority to him, she harbors no objections to his position.
*   **Xie Ling:** Her junior brother, whose smooth cultivation journey and immense fortune she sometimes envies.
*   **Liu Xianyang:** Her junior brother and the current Sect Master, whose flippant and teasing nature greatly annoys her.
