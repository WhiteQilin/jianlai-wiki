## Overview

Cui Dongshan (崔东山) is a disciple of [Chen Ping'an](/characters/chen-pingan) and the first sect master of the [Qingping Sword Sect](/factions/qingping-sword-sect). He carries a splintered portion of the soul of [Cui Chan](/characters/cui-chan), the former National Teacher of the Great Li Dynasty, which underpins his exceptional intelligence and strategic foresight. Outwardly he presents as a flamboyant, white-robed eccentric, but this persona conceals deep calculation and an intense devotion to his master.

## Personality

Cui Dongshan is best known for his theatrical, often childish public behavior, which he uses to disarm rivals and mask his true intentions. Beneath the act he is a meticulous schemer who consistently works to advance Chen Ping'an's interests and to protect [Luopo Mountain](/factions/luopo-mountain). His loyalty to Chen Ping'an is a defining trait, frequently expressed through exaggerated declarations of admiration.

## Affiliations

- **[Luopo Mountain](/factions/luopo-mountain):** A core member of Chen Ping'an's mountain estate.
- **[Qingping Sword Sect](/factions/qingping-sword-sect):** Serves as its first sect master.
