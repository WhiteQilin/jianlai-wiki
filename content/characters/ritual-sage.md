## Overview

The **Ritual Sage** (礼圣) is one of the supreme defining sages of the Confucian Temple (文庙) in the Haoran Heaven, second in authority only to the Most Holy Teacher (至圣先师). He is the primary architect of the foundational rules, moral frameworks, and laws that govern both the secular dynasties and the mountain cultivation sects of the human world. 

To protect the world from apocalyptic threats, the Ritual Sage permanently stations himself in the outer heavens (天外), standing as an absolute bulwark against the remnants of ancient high-tier gods. Because of his immense responsibility and staggering power, he rarely interferes in minor worldly disputes, but whenever he descends to preside over major assemblies, his sheer authority commands absolute silence and obedience from even the oldest and most arrogant patriarchs of the Hundred Schools of Thought.

## Authority & Governance

The Ritual Sage represents the absolute enforcement of the Haoran Heaven's laws. 

*   **The Hundred Schools of Thought:** During pivotal mid-war and post-war Confucian Temple assemblies, the Ritual Sage personally oversees the evaluation, promotion, and demotion of the various philosophical and martial lineages (诸子百家). His unquestionable status forces patriarchs who might otherwise complain or bargain to remain completely silent and accept his rulings.
*   **The Ultimate Deterrent:** In the modern era, as apex cultivators like the White Emperor City Lord Zheng Juzhong navigate the limits of the world's laws, the Ritual Sage is widely recognized by top-tier entities as the *only* existence capable of truly constraining and restraining Zheng Juzhong's actions. 
*   **The Riverside Assembly:** In preparation for the ultimate clashes involving the Manhuang Heaven and the Outer Heavens, the Ritual Sage acted as the primary host and "chief examiner" (主考官) of a highly secretive riverside assembly. This gathering pulled together a large number of the universe's 14th-realm cultivators across three worlds, evaluating them in preparation for the cosmic shifts caused by the dispersing of the Three Teachings' patriarchs.

## History & Legacy

In the ancient era, the Ritual Sage worked alongside the Most Holy Teacher to break through the primordial ignorance of humanity, establishing the original rites and laws that shaped human civilization. 

*   **The Ancient Reform (改制):** Historical records indicate that long ago, the Ritual Sage attempted a massive ideological and structural reform of the world's systems. Although this specific reform ultimately failed, it fundamentally shifted the trajectory of the Haoran Heaven's cultural and academic lineage for millennia.
*   **Authorship:** The Ritual Sage authored foundational classical texts that are meticulously studied by subsequent generations of Confucian scholars. His writings frequently serve as the ultimate orthodox standard for philology and historical exegesis, with phrases like "修火禁" actively debated and cited by scholars to prove proper grammatical and historical context.
