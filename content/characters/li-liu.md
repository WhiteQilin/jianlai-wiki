## Overview

Li Liu (李柳) is the daughter of the martial artist Li Er and the older sister of Li Huai, originally born in the Lizhu Grotto-Heaven. Though she appears to be a gentle, docile mortal woman who passively accepts her mother's scolding, she is actually born with innate knowledge (生而知之) of her past lives. Her true identity is the reincarnation of the ancient Jianghu Co-lord (江湖共主), one of the five highest ancient gods who commanded the world's waters and the dragons.

After leaving her hometown, she became a cultivator at Shizi Peak in Beiju Luzhou, eventually reaching the Immortal Realm (仙人境) with unprecedented speed. Ultimately, to avoid repeating an apocalyptic ancient water-fire dispute, she chose to strip away her divine nature, preferring to live as a human alongside her mortal family.

## Appearance and Personality

Li Liu is described as a beautiful young woman with a goose-egg face and autumn-water eyes. She inherited her mother's good looks but possesses her father's accommodating, quiet temperament. In her daily life, she is often seen performing mundane chores, fetching water, or sewing cloth shoes for her brother, always wearing a soft, tranquil smile. 

However, her gentle exterior masks the profound detachment of an ancient high god. Having lived through countless lifetimes and reincarnations, she views the world's life and death, as well as the struggles of cultivators, with immense indifference. The only things she truly values are her human family—her father, her nagging mother, and especially her younger brother, Li Huai.

## True Identity and Divine Authority

In the ancient era, Li Liu was the Jianghu Co-lord, one of the five highest deities of the old Heavenly Court. She held supreme authority over the world's waters, rivers, lakes, and seas. Even the powerful Dragon Slaying Envoys (斩龙使), who supervised and commanded the world's dragons, answered directly to her. 

In her past, she engaged in a catastrophic Great Dao dispute of water and fire with the Fire God (the predecessor of Ruan Xiu), a conflict that Li Liu ultimately won. To temper her divine nature and fulfill a complex arrangement orchestrated by Yang Laotou, she was reincarnated into the Li family in Lizhu Grotto-Heaven.

## History and Cultivation

After leaving Lizhu Grotto-Heaven, Li Liu traveled to Beiju Luzhou with her parents. Through the arrangements of Yang Laotou and the swordsman Cao Xi, she ascended Shizi Peak (狮子峰). Cao Xi handed over a golden lion seal and his old natal short sword to her, and with a single swing of the sword, she unleashed a heaven-cleaving sword aura, instantly breaking into the Middle Five Realms. She eventually reclaimed her position as the true founding ancestor of Shizi Peak's hidden lineage.

Her divine authority over water allowed her to perform miraculous feats effortlessly. When visiting the Dragon Palace Grotto-Heaven, she casually repaired the shattering golden body of the water deity Shen Lin and decreed that Shen Lin would become the Jidu Lingyuan Gong. Later, at Xie Long Stone (歇龙石), she stomped her foot and shattered the entire mountain into the sea to punish Liu Chicheng for his insolence, simultaneously forcing the Ascension Realm demon Dandan Furen (澹澹夫人) of Lushui Pit to submit as her servant.

Within a few short years, Li Liu reached the Immortal Realm (仙人境). Despite her immense power, she recognized that her divine nature would eventually overshadow her humanity, causing her to lose her emotional attachment to her family. When the time came for a final resolution with Ruan Xiu, Li Liu chose not to fight. Instead, she voluntarily stripped away all her divine nature and discarded it, allowing Ruan Xiu to ascend to the sky. Li Liu's discarded divine nature was later acquired by the sword cultivator Yu Si.

## Relationships

*   **Li Er and Mother:** Li Liu is a filial daughter who never talks back to her mother's constant complaining. Her father, Li Er, understands her true nature but simply treats her as his beloved daughter, hoping she retains her human affections.
*   **Li Huai:** Her younger brother. Li Liu dotes on him deeply. Li Huai is fiercely protective of his sister, frequently threatening anyone he perceives as trying to court her, though he is entirely unaware of her supreme divine status.
*   **Han Chengjiang (韩澄江):** Her husband. A lower-tier cultivator from the Han clan of the Hualing Dynasty. Their marriage was orchestrated by Yang Laotou to settle karmic debts and intertwined destinies from two of Han Chengjiang's previous lifetimes in the Middle Earth Divine Continent and Liuxia Continent.
*   **Ruan Xiu:** The reincarnation of the ancient Fire God. They are ancient rivals who fought a world-shaking water-fire dispute in the past. In this lifetime, they maintained a tense, cold relationship until Li Liu gave up her divine nature to avoid another cataclysmic battle.
*   **Yang Laotou:** The ancient Qingtóng Heavenly Lord who oversaw her reincarnation and guided her human tempering in the Lizhu Grotto-Heaven, warning her that merely accumulating lifetimes without understanding humanity would leave her a flawed god.
*   **Chen Ping'an:** Though Lin Shouyi and Dong Shuijing harbored childhood crushes on her, Li Liu felt no romantic attachment to anyone from her hometown. She views Chen Ping'an with respect for how he treated Li Huai, referring to him politely as "Mr. Chen" (陈先生).
