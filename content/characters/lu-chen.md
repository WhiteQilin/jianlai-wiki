## Overview

**Lu Chen** (陆沉) is the Third Headmaster (三掌教) of Bai Yujing (白玉京) and the third direct disciple of the Dao Ancestor (道祖). Existing at the absolute pinnacle of the Qingming Heaven, he is a 14th-Realm entity whose power and philosophical mastery are universally acknowledged across multiple worlds. 

Unlike his incredibly strict and martial-minded senior brother Yu Dou (余斗), Lu Chen is famous for his deeply eccentric, carefree, and seemingly lazy demeanor. He frequently leaves the Qingming Heaven to wander the Haoran Heaven and other realms under various guises, most notably adopting the persona of a wandering fortune-teller or a humble Daoist priest. Despite his outwardly frivolous nature, he possesses an unfathomable understanding of the Great Dao and is considered one of the most intellectually profound figures in the universe.

## Personality & Governance

Lu Chen's approach to governance is notoriously relaxed, standing in stark contrast to the iron-fisted rule of Yu Dou. Bai Yujing is governed by the Dao Ancestor's three disciples in rotating centuries. When it is Lu Chen's turn to oversee the Qingming Heaven, he famously refuses to micromanage the Daoist sects. 

His philosophy heavily emphasizes natural progression, non-interference, and observing the world as if it were a dream. When he first became the Dao Ancestor's youngest disciple, he once confidently boasted to the visiting Chunyang Zhenren (吕喦) that the Dao naturally began with their master, passed to his senior brothers, but would reach its absolute peak of flourishing incense under him, and that he would eventually return this magnificent spectacle to the world.

## History & Major Events

### Origins & Departure from Haoran Heaven
Although he is the supreme ruler of the Daoist Qingming Heaven, Lu Chen was originally born in the Haoran Heaven. Describing his own path, he stated that he grew "tired of the world after a thousand years" (千岁厌世). Refusing to remain in the Haoran Heaven, he chose to change his environment, journeying on white clouds to the "Emperor's Home" (帝乡)—the Qingming Heaven—where he believed his Dao heart could truly find peace. 

### The Buddha's Test
In his past, Lu Chen was once pulled into a profound and mysterious "Great Thousand World" (大千世界) illusion by the Buddha. Within this illusion, Lu Chen experienced what felt like tens of thousands of years, a profound test of his Dao heart and his perception of reality.

### The Lizhu Grotto-Heaven
During the final years of the Lizhu Grotto-Heaven, Lu Chen traveled to the small town and set up a fortune-telling stall in Xinghua Alley. Establishing a fortune-telling stall in a place where heavenly secrets were so fiercely contested was an incredibly difficult and thankless task. There, he closely observed the town's inhabitants, including a young Chen Ping'an, occasionally offering cryptic advice, manipulation, or judgment to figures like Zhu He, testing their natures before the grotto-heaven fell to earth. 

### The Riverside Pact & Manhuang Aftermath
During the wars involving the Manhuang Heaven, Lu Chen traveled to the demon world, originally intending only to escort the rogue Penal Official Hao Su (豪素) back to Qingming. Instead, he ended up boarding Chen Ping'an's "pirate ship." Later, due to the complex cosmological calculations of Zou Zi involving the Daoist Xin Ku crossing worlds into Manhuang, Lu Chen ended up bearing the immense burden of two "Heavenly Disgusts" (天厌) in the open and in the dark, acting as a shield for his senior brother Yu Dou.

## Cultivation & Abilities

Lu Chen has reached the absolute consummation of the 14th Realm. His mastery extends to almost every auxiliary path of cultivation; he is a peerless expert in divination, talismans, and observing the karmic threads of the world.

*   **Five Dreams and Seven Heart Phases (五梦七心相):** A profound and terrifying manifestation of his Dao. Lu Chen scattered fragments of his consciousness across the worlds. These "phases" lived entirely independent lives, unaware they were part of Lu Chen, acting as anchors for his cultivation. Before visiting Chen Ping'an at the Sword Qi Great Wall, Lu Chen purposefully recalled "one dream and one heart phase"—which included the peerless martial artist Yu Zhenyi (俞真意) from the Lotus Root Blessed Land and the Confucian scholar Zheng Huan (郑缓)—granting them true freedom rather than keeping them as his puppets. 
*   **Illusion & Reality:** Lu Chen's Dao heavily blurs the line between the observer and the observed. There is a famous saying in the Xuan Du Temple regarding him: "Lu Chen is not a true person. Everything seen in his eyes is not reality." (陆沉此人，不是真人。眼中所见，都非真实).

## Relationships

*   **The Dao Ancestor (道祖):** His master. Lu Chen deeply respects the Dao Ancestor's philosophy of non-action and often acts as a casual observer of the universe's shifting tides.
*   **Yu Dou (余斗):** His second senior brother. The two are polar opposites in personality and governance style. Lu Chen often exasperates Yu Dou with his lack of discipline.
*   **Chen Ping'an (陈平安):** Lu Chen has crossed paths with the young Hidden Official numerous times, beginning in the mud alleys of Chen Ping'an's hometown. Their relationship is highly complex—a mix of grudging respect, philosophical opposition, and mutual utilization.
